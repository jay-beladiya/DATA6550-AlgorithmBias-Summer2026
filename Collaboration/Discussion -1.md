### Discussion

We started by reviewing the project instructions to understand what needed to be completed. One of the first questions we had was whether we should analyze the entire mortgage dataset or separate the work into different sections. After looking at the project requirements, we decided to divide the work based on the two main topics in the report.

We agreed that one section would focus on data bias by examining historical housing patterns, including property values, neighborhood income, minority population, and covenant density. The other section would focus on algorithmic bias by looking at lending outcomes such as loan approvals, interest rates, and denial reasons.

Another question we discussed was which datasets should be used. We noticed that the mortgage covenant dataset already included the historical covenant information needed for the bias analysis, while the original Minnesota HMDA dataset would be more useful later for the Ramsey County comparison.

By the end of the discussion, we agreed on the project structure, assigned responsibilities for each section, and decided to keep the coding style and notebook organization consistent across both parts.
